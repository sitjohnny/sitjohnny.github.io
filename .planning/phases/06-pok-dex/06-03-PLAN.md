## Phase Goal

**As a** child catching Pokémon on Safari, **I want to** open a Gen 1 Pokédex where unknowns stay silhouettes until I catch them and my Seen/Caught progress is visible, **so that** the endless loop has a collection reward that drives me back into the grass.

**This plan's slice:** wire explore→encounter→capture into the dex store so catches lift silhouettes, Seen ticks on appear (including flees), shiny becomes reachable, and progress debounces into SAVE_KEY (DEX-02 + DEX-01 reveal).

<objective>
Add config-driven shinyRate + dexSaveDebounceMs, extend EncounterSession with shiny, roll shiny at session open with injected Rng, bind markSeen after open and recordCatch on Gotcha in useEncounterFlow, thread shiny through overlay sprites, and finish dexStore debounced + visibilitychange/pagehide flush (D-06, D-09, D-19, D-20 storage, D-21).

Purpose: Collection progress becomes a live reward of the capture loop — the vertical slice that makes the Pokédex react to play.
Output: green useEncounterFlow dex assertions, config-surface shiny/debounce keys, dexStore debounce tests; catching updates /dex tiles and counters.
</objective>
