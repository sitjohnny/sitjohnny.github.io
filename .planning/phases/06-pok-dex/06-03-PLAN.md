## Phase Goal

**As a** child catching Pokémon on Safari, **I want to** open a Gen 1 Pokédex where unknowns stay silhouettes until I catch them and my Seen/Caught progress is visible, **so that** the endless loop has a collection reward that drives me back into the grass.

**This plan's slice:** the first playable Pokédex — open `/dex`, browse 151 silhouettes under a sticky completion header, tap for a stub detail. Encounter writes land in `06-04`; caught lore lands in `06-06`.

> **Split note (scope-sanity gate):** this is the browse half of the original 17-file `06-02`. It consumes the store and selectors built in `06-02` and needs nothing from later plans, so it is end-to-end playable the moment it lands (with a seeded or empty dex).

<objective>
Build the Dex browse surface on top of the `06-02` data plane: silhouette support in `PokemonSprite`, the dex CSS utilities, `DexTile`/`DexGrid`/`DexHeader`, a leak-free `DexDetailSheet` stub, and a `DexScreen` that replaces the `EmptyState` placeholder (D-01, D-02, D-04, D-07 – D-13).

Purpose: the collection becomes visible — the phase's user-facing payoff exists before any encounter wiring.
Output: green `DexScreen.test.tsx` stub path and `DexTile.test.tsx` unknown/caught assertions; live `/dex` UI.
</objective>
