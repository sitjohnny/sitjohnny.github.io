## Phase Goal

**As a** child catching Pokémon on Safari, **I want to** open a Gen 1 Pokédex where unknowns stay silhouettes until I catch them and my Seen/Caught progress is visible, **so that** the endless loop has a collection reward that drives me back into the grass.

**This plan's slice:** first end-to-end browse path — open `/dex`, see 151 silhouettes + sticky completion, tap stub detail. Encounter→dex writes and flavor lore land in `06-03` / `06-04`.

</objective>
